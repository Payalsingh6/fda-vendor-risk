## PL-LIAB-001 Limitation of Liability
Standard: Liability capped at 2x annual contract value.
Fallback: 1x annual value acceptable.
Walk-away: Unlimited liability not acceptable.
Rule ID: PL-LIAB-001
Clause Type: limitation_of_liability

## PL-INDEM-001 Indemnity
Standard: Vendor indemnifies for its gross negligence and willful misconduct only.
Fallback: Mutual indemnity for breach of confidentiality.
Walk-away: Indemnifies for all claims.
Rule ID: PL-INDEM-001
Clause Type: indemnity

## PL-TERM-001 Termination
Standard: Termination for convenience with 30 days notice.
Rule ID: PL-TERM-001
Clause Type: termination

## PL-AUTO-001 Auto-renewal

Rule ID: PL-AUTO-001
